From match to market

One harvest.
Six accountable moments.

Every handoff creates a record, a decision, or a measurable outcome.

  1. 01
    List the land

    A landowner documents location, area, soil, and availability.

  2. 02
    Match a farmer

    A verified farmer receives and accepts a lease proposal.

  3. 03
    Approve the plan

    The administrator reviews the crop, duration, and required budget.

  4. 04
    Fund the work

    Investors contribute only up to the remaining approved budget.

  5. 05
    Track the field

    The farmer records progress, expenses, completion, and harvest value.

  6. 06
    Share the profit

    A transactional settlement allocates positive profit exactly once.
